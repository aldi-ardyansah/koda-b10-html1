Kafkaesque

Kafkaesque is an adjective that describes situations, environments, or systems that are bizarre, surreal, illogically complex, and nightmarishly bureaucratic.

  • Marked by a senseless, disorienting, often menacing complexity.
  • Kafkaesque bureaucracies.
  • Marked by surreal distortion and often a sense of looming danger. In the manner of something written by Franz Kafka.
